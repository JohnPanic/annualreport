Letter from Leadership

Adapting with gratitude, hope, and purpose.

Dear Friends,

BBYO has spent more than a century adapting to moments of change. This year, that meant redirecting teen travel, deepening global connection, and continuing to build joyful Jewish life in communities around the world.

From International Convention in Denver to summer experiences across Europe, Israel, and North America, teens found ways to lead with resilience, curiosity, and pride.
