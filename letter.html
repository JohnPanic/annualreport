Letter from the Showcase

A Movement built to adapt.

Full Letter

With gratitude and hope

Dear Friends,

Every great organization tells a story worth telling, and the best ones find new ways to tell it every year.

This annual report is an exercise in exactly that. Built as a portfolio showcase, EXHB.01 demonstrates what's possible when design, data, and storytelling come together in a single digital experience. The format, long a staple of institutional communication, has enormous untapped creative potential.

What you'll find here: a year in numbers that actually feels alive, leadership profiles with presence, program narratives that breathe, and a reach section that earns its place on the page.

This isn't a real organization's report. It's a proof of concept: that annual reports don't have to be PDFs nobody reads. They can be experiences people actually want to explore.

Thank you for spending time with it.

With gratitude and intention,
